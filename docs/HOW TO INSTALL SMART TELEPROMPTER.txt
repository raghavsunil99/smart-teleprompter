SMART TELEPROMPTER BY RAGHAV — HOW TO INSTALL
=============================================

Smart Teleprompter listens while you talk and scrolls your script along with you.
It works with scripts in Telugu letters, Tenglish (Telugu typed in English letters)
and English.

You need:
  • an Android phone or tablet (Android 8 or newer)
  • the Google app (for Telugu speech recognition) and usually internet
  • optional: a laptop with Google Chrome on the same Wi-Fi, to use as a remote and mic


1. INSTALL ON ANDROID
---------------------
1. Copy  Smart-Teleprompter-by-Raghav-1.5.apk  to the phone (WhatsApp it to yourself,
   Google Drive, or a USB cable) and tap it.
2. Android asks whether to allow installing apps from that source (Chrome, Files, WhatsApp…).
   Tap  Settings  → turn on  "Allow from this source"  → go back → Install.
3. If Play Protect warns that the app is unknown, tap  More details  →  Install anyway.
4. Open "Teleprompter" from your apps (the icon is a small prompter screen).
5. The first time you press ▶, allow the microphone.

Updating from an earlier version: install the new file over the old one. Your scripts stay.


2. FIRST RECORDING
------------------
1. Tap a script (a sample is included), or tap  New  and paste your own.
2. Press ▶. After the 3-2-1 countdown, start talking. The script follows your voice.
3. Pause by tapping the screen, then ▶ (or use a Bluetooth remote).
4. Lost your place? Long-press a word to continue from there.


3. IN A TELEPROMPTER RIG
------------------------
1. Tap  Prompter  in the bottom bar: the text flips for the glass and the screen locks
   to landscape. Tap it again to undo.
2. If the text still looks wrong in the glass, open  Settings  and try
   "Mirror left-right" instead.


4. LAPTOP REMOTE (OPTIONAL)
---------------------------
1. Put the laptop on the same Wi-Fi as the phone (or on the phone's hotspot).
2. In the app tap  Remote  → switch Remote on. It shows two addresses.
3. On the laptop, open the  https://…:8443  address in Google Chrome.
   The first time Chrome says "Your connection is not private":
   click  Advanced  →  Proceed. It's your own phone.
4. Click  Listen on laptop  and allow the microphone to let the laptop do the listening
   (useful when the phone is far away in a rig).

If the laptop can't connect: office and hotel Wi-Fi often block devices from talking to
each other. Use the phone's hotspot instead.
